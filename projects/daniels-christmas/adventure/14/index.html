You can restock some of your supplies and maybe spring for a hotel so you can rest in a bed. Wouldn’t it be great to eat something besides Clif bars for a change? As you get closer and closer to the lights you start dreaming of the warm food you will eat and wondering what this town so far north might have to offer.

You get closer to downtown and you start to smell smoke and you begin to get an uneasy feeling. You hear shouting and several police cars fly past you, heading towards the commotion. As you get closer you can hear the chants and it sounds like you have stumbled upon a protest. While there are some disturbing sounds coming from several blocks away, the crowd where you are is peaceful and friendly. They tell you about their cause and it’s something you care about passionately as well. Do you:

  1. Take the sign someone is handing to you and join in the protest?
  2. Turn on to a quiet street and make your way out of town?
