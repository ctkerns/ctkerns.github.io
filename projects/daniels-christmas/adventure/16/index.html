There are no people on this street, but the sirens tell you that things are heating up. As you walk down the street glass crunches under your feet and you see evidence all around of recent looting. You decide to help yourself to the food and drop some spare change near the smashed tip jar. Who is paying attention in the middle of a riot anyway? Not the police, for sure, they are busy.
