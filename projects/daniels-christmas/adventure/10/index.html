You sling your backpack over your shoulder, put on your mask and head out the door. Before too long the lights of the city are well behind you and you are glad to enjoy the open skies and the broad landscapes that lay before you.

The weather is getting colder and you have switched your street shoes to your boots so your feet are nice and toasty. Your mask is keeping your face warm. Unfortunately, you left behind the Clif bars and you are starting to get hungry. You are pretty sure there is a general store in the next town, so you press on, hoping to buy a snack when you get there.

Sure enough, when you get to the town, there IS a store and, as your stomach begins to rumble loudly, you pick up the pace to get inside. Once inside you are thrilled to see shelves and shelves of snacks and your mouth starts to water. You pick your favorite, and then your second favorite and third favorite, and since your hands are full, you tuck your fourth favorite into your pocket as you head to the register. While at the register, you realize you forgot to grab something to drink. You leave the snacks on the counter and head back to grab some hot cocoa for now and some water for later.

As you walk out the door, you realize that you forgot to pay for the 4th favorite snack in your pocket. The store manager, however, realized your mistake before you did. He signaled to the sheriff’s deputy who just happened to be walking into the store for his daily cup of cocoa. Next thing you know you are being booked for shoplifting and you are certain your name is on the Naughty List. No chance of getting Santa to hear your appeal this year.
