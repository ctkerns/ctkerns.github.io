You walk and walk, careful not to trip over the still scampering woodland creatures that seem to be overjoyed at your company. Maybe it’s because you are still whistling the familiar family favorites and that is attracting them. Despite the tripping hazard, they are cheerful and you are glad to have them along your journey.

The forest starts to thin and as you come to the edge of the forest you see a little cottage and some outlying buildings. There are lights blazing in the windows and smoke wafting from the chimney. You are almost overcome with relief! Almost, but not quite. With the animals to cheer you, you persevere and knock tentatively at the door.

What joy when the door opens and you see Mrs. Claus’ face peer out at you! Her expression rapidly changes to one of concern and you are quickly pulled in out of the cold and set in a comfy chair by the fire. Fatigue wins over hunger and soon you are napping. When you awake, on the table there is a plate of nourishing food waiting for you. As soon as your eyes start to open an elf rushes a cup of warm cocoa over to you. In the opposite chair is Santa himself, with a look of concern on his face.

He hears your appeal and says:

My, you have been on a journey! And it is clear how much you love and value your family! You are obviously on the Nice list and deserving of an extra special Christmas. I cannot rid the world of Covid in time for you all to be together this year, but I can give you this. He hands you a small present.
