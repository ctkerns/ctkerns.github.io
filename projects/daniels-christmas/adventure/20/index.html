Although your brain is hurting from the recent blow to the head and the obvious cognitive dissonance of a talking snowman, you push through and hail the friendly looking fellow. Is he a fellow? Who knows? It’s 2020 and you’ve already adapted to so many things that even addressing gender fluid snow creatures seems like something you can roll with at this point. And they seem quite friendly.

With their stick arm they indicate a gap in the trees you had not noticed before and you see a path through the woods. You thank the snow creature profusely and start down the path.
