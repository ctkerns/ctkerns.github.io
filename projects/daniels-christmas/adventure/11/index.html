You sling your backpack over your shoulder, put on your mask and head out the door. Before too long the lights of the city are well behind you and you are glad to enjoy the open skies and the broad landscapes that lay before you. You are a little worried that you left the parka behind, but there’s global warming, right? Polar ice caps are melting, right? Bad for the planet but good for your travel fashion choices.

The weather is getting colder and you have switched your street shoes to your boots so your feet are nice and toasty. Your mask is keeping your face warm. You’ve got Grandma’s hat on your head and her gloves on your hands and the thought of her warms your heart.

If only you had grabbed the parka as well. Too late you remember the lesson that global warming is a sign of climate change, which includes not just warming but more extreme weather in general. And this year, climate change means a severe blizzard hits you before you make your first camp.

It becomes painfully clear that you will not be able to survive the frigid temperatures with your current clothes. You make the wise, but disappointing choice to turn back home and hope that Santa can receive your message telepathically.
