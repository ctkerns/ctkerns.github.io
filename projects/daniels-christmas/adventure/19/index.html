You wake up in a snow bank. Your head still hurts from the tree branch, but otherwise you seem OK. You stand up and look around at a strange new world.

The trees look funny. What’s with their trunks? They are striped! Red and white! Like….Candy Canes! You are in the Candy Cane Forest!! Charming little woodland creatures scamper happily around your feet. You look down at your compass and the dial is spinning. You must be close to the North Pole, but which way to Santa’s Workshop? You see a round figure in the distance. It looks like a snowman and he’s headed your way, though you aren’t sure how because he has no legs. And he’s made of snow. Do you:

  1. Greet the snowman and ask for directions?
  2. Trust your instincts and follow the northern lights?
