You write your letter and head to the mailbox on the corner. When you get there, you are surprised to find it isn’t there anymore. That’s OK, because you know there is another one a few blocks away. You could use the extra steps. But when you get there, that mailbox isn’t there anymore either! What?! This is a main street and you are convinced that there must be a mailbox on it. Do you:

  1. Keep walking until you find a mailbox?
  2. Go back home and try to find another way to get your letter to Santa?
