Persimmon Pudding

Ingredients:

  • 1 Tbs. butter softened
  • 2 Tbs. butter melted and cooled
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 tsp. cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup walnuts, optional
  • 1/2 tsp. ginger
  • 1 cup persimmons, peeled & pureed
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 tsp. vanilla

Preheat the oven to 350º. Spread butter over bottom and sides of 1 quart dish. Combine flour, soda, cinnamon, ginger, and nutmeg. Set aside.

Add sugar to persimmon puree and mix well. Stir in flour mixture and milk in small portions until well mixed. Add melted butter and vanilla. Pour into buttered dish. Bake in oven for 50 to 60 minutes, or until pudding begins to shrink away from the sides of the dish and a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. Serves 4-6 (8 x fills my oven) Soak in ½ cup brandy and ignite to serve.
