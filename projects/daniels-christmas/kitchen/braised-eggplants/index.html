Braised Eggplants with Soy, Garlic, and Ginger

Here is a contribution from Terre to counter all the sweet. A crowd pleasing eggplant recipe:


Ingredients:

  • 1.5 Cups of water
  • 1/4 cup Chinese rice or dry sherry (we use dry wine if that is all we have)
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 4 teaspoons of sugar
  • 2 teaspoons of Asian broad-bean chili paste
  • 1 teaspoon corn starch
  • two globe or Italian eggplants
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 garlic clove minced
  • 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
  • half teaspoon roasted sesame oil
  • two scallions sliced thin on the bias

1. Whisk water, rice wine, soy sauce, sugar, chili paste, and corn starch in medium bowl until sugar is dissolved. Trim half inch from top and bottom of eggplant. Halve eggplant crosswise. Cut each half lengthwise into two pieces. Cut each piece into three-quarter inch thick wedges. Repeat with remaining eggplant.

2. Heat vegetable oil in 12-inch nonstick skillet over medium heat until simmering. Add garlic and ginger and cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Spread eggplant evenly in the skillet pieces will not form a single layer. Pour rice wine mixture over eggplant. Increase heat to high and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to maintain gentle boil. Cover and cook until eggplant is soft and has decreased in volume enough to form a single layer on the bottom of the skillet. Gently shake skillet to settle eggplant halfway through cooking.

3. Uncover and continue to cook swirling skillet occasionally, until liquid is thickened and reduced to just a few tablespoons. Transfer to platter, drizzle with sesame oil, sprinkle with scallions, and enjoy!
