Rum Pie

I (Julia) found this in my mother’s recipes. Her Greek neighbor (Jeannie Christopolous) typed several recipes out to share with her friend and this was among them. I have no idea if the pie is any good, but her editorial comment mid recipe made me giggle. Apparently she had been sampling the rum as she typed 😊:


Curst (whoops() that was cru st but what the hell…it’s very late and I’m as rummy the pie! NOW… the crust:

  • 18 finely crumbled graham crackers
  • 1/3 c. sugar
  • Dash cinnamon
  • ¼ puund melted butter.

Filling:

  • 4 small pkgs. Cream cheese
  • ½ c. sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 ounze rum

Put all this in a bowl and let your electric mixer beat the daylights out of it… till nice and thick as cream. Pour into crust and bake 20 min. in 375 oven. Cool the result for a few minutes (5… 10…) and then spread the following on top and bake 65 minutes longer.

  • 1 c. sour cream
  • 3 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 tablespoon sherry

VERY EASY… HEAP BIG SUCCESS

(Much better made the day before)
